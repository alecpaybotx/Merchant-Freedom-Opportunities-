Cash Discount Program

Card customers pay the posted price. Cash customers receive a 2 to 4% discount. Your processing fees drop to zero. This is a legal, widely adopted program that thousands of merchants use nationwide.

Lower Processing Rates

If a cash discount program isn't right for your business, we negotiate lower interchange rates directly. We compare your current statement line by line and show you what's possible before you commit to anything.

Free Equipment

POS terminals, handhelds, kitchen display systems, printers. All free. No upfront cost, no leasing traps, no hidden fees. Equipment financed and covered through our agent structure.

Local Agent Support

You get Alec's personal number. Text or call any time. No call centers, no tickets, no waiting on hold. A real person who knows your account, your business, and your goals.

No Contracts, No Minimums

No long-term commitments, no cancellation fees, no monthly minimums, no statement fees. You stay because it works, not because you're locked in. Transparent from day one.

E-Commerce Solutions
